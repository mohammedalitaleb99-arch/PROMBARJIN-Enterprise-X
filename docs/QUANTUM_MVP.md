# PROMBARJIN Ω Quantum MVP

This branch adds a local state-vector quantum simulator to the existing PROMBARJIN Enterprise X application.

## Truth boundary

This is a **classical simulation of quantum logic**. It is not quantum hardware and does not establish quantum advantage.

## Initial demonstrations

- Bell state: `H(q0) -> CNOT(q0,q1)`
- GHZ state: `H(q0)` followed by controlled-X operations
- Probability and sampled measurement distributions

## Qubit convention

`q0` is the least-significant bit (LSB).
